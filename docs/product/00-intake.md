# Intake & Framing
**Product:** FirsThing Platform · **Phase:** 0 — Intake · **Status:** Approved
**Last updated:** 2026-08-12 (Phase 3 gate: CON-01c/01d, CON-34–37 added; CON-24/CON-25 materially corrected. Post-gate audit: CON-38/39, ASSUM-21/22. Phase 4: CON-40/41, ASSUM-23) · **Mode:** Ecosystem

---

## 1. One-paragraph summary

FirsThing sells **verified utility savings** to residential societies (apartment complexes / RWAs)
in India. The commercial model is a savings share: at contract signing a **benchmark** is
established, and each month the actual metered performance is compared against it. If the month
lands within the **contracted tolerance band** of the benchmark (per-contract, commonly ±5% or
±10% — CON-01a), the society is billed the pre-agreed fixed amount. If it falls outside that band
in either direction, FirsThing investigates whether the deviation is
fixable; if it is not, the monthly bill is adjusted accordingly. Lighting (20–500 lights per
*circuit*, many circuits per society — CON-07) is the established service line; **water pumps** are already in delivery, and **solar
generation** and **wastewater management** are being entered. The platform must therefore stop
being a lighting dashboard and become a **multi-service savings and generation platform**, where a
society sees one cumulative view of energy saved, energy generated, and financial benefit, with
per-service detail behind it.

This blueprint exists because the application, as built, has no fixed direction — it accreted
screens without a settled product model, and its design-handoff prototype and its shipped code now
describe materially different products for 5 of 6 admin modules.

## 2. Scope shape

- **Type:** Rebuild-by-evolution. The blueprint is authored first and drives the work; the existing
  `newUI` codebase is the substrate that gets reshaped toward it, not a constraint on what the plan
  may say. Working foundations (NextAuth/Prisma/Postgres, S3 storage, Gemini extraction) are
  expected to survive; information architecture and data model are explicitly open.
- **Branch:** `blueprint`, cut from `newUI` at `760a4e0`.

### Surfaces

| Surface | Purpose | Primary roles | Status today |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SUR-01 — Web application** | Back-office operations, society-facing portal | FirsThing admin/ops, FirsThing customer support, society committee, society manager | Built, partially migrated, IA contested |
| **SUR-02 — Inspector field surface** | On-site inspection capture, fault logging, spare-light reconciliation, initial installation/commissioning (benchmark setup) | Field inspector, installer/commissioning | Built as desktop web screens; design bundle mocks it as a phone app; still Supabase-backed |

Ingest of meter data is treated as a **capability of SUR-01**, not a third designed surface.
OQ-04 is resolved: ingest is manual CSV upload only for this blueprint (CON-30), so no third
surface is warranted. Live telemetry is a named future phase (ASSUM-13); if it ever lands, ingest
is promoted to its own surface then.

### Service lines

| ID | Service line | Rebuild treatment | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| SVC-01 | Lighting | **Build** | Established; 20–500 lights per society; the benchmark model is proven here |
| SVC-02 | Water pumps | **Build** | Already in commercial delivery; first real test of the multi-service abstraction |

### Explicitly out of scope

Confirmed 2026-08-10:

- No resident/flat-level application. The society is the customer; individual residents are not users.
- No payment collection or checkout inside the product. Invoices are documents; money moves outside.
- No hardware or firmware design. Meters and sensors are inputs.
- No accounting-system integration (Tally, Zoho, etc.) at launch.

### Explicitly in scope (resolved 2026-08-10, previously open)

- **Supabase→Prisma cutover** (inspection module, energy chart, `FileUploader.tsx` — 11 files) is
  part of this blueprint's backlog, not tracked separately.
- **Ticketing** (issue logging/triage/resolution) and **spare-light inventory** are new capability
  areas with full Phase 3-6 treatment, not deferred.
- **Meter data ingest is manual CSV upload only for this blueprint.** Live device telemetry is an
  explicit, named future phase (user's term: "phase 2") — captured as a roadmap note, not designed
  or built here. If revisited, it likely promotes ingest from a SUR-01 capability to its own surface.
- **Inspector field surface is mobile web** (responsive, same Next.js stack), not a native app.
- **FirsThing owns and installs the hardware** (CON-08): lighting fully, pump automation
  (VFD/motor controllers, contactors, pressure switches, water-level sensors, actuator valves)
  partially. Hardware asset tracking/ownership belongs in the Phase 3 data model.
- **Platform is monitor-only toward pump automation hardware** (INV-08) — no remote
  actuation/control commands in this blueprint; sensor/status reads only.
- **Customer support staff need their own app access** (a 6th persona, Phase 2) — dispute status,
  communication logs, ticket state. Full detail in `02-users-research.md`.
- **Non-payment can trigger full society suspension**, including servicing, not just app-login
  lockout (CON-13). Trigger threshold and approval mechanics are resolved (ASSUM-14, validated);
  suspension halts field servicing only — ingest, billing, and portal access continue.
- **Basic CSV-upload-time anomaly detection is in scope** (INV-09); the full real-time
  notification/email/communication-log/ignore-tracking system described by the user is deferred
  alongside live telemetry (ASSUM-13), not built now.
